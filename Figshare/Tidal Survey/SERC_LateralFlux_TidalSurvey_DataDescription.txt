These data are measurements of dissolved inorganic carbon, dissolved organic carbon, and total dissolved nitrogen concentrations were collected from surface waters, marsh porewaters, and upland groundwaters at the Smithsonian Environmental Research Center's Global Change Research Wetland (GCReW) in the Kirkpatrick Marsh along the Rhode River in Edgewater, MD, USA. Samples were collected roughly bi-weekly at random time points throuhout 2025. The surface waters were collected at the GCReW Flume through which a tidal creek flows, marsh porewaters were collected in 1m fully screen wells, and upland groundwater wells were up to 5m deep. Samples were collected by hand or with an ISCO automated water sampler. When collected by hand (surface, pore and groundwaters) were filtered immediately with 0.45 um syringe filters into combusted glass vials and kept on ice transport to fridge or freezer. When collected by ISCO, samples were held on ice inside the ISCO for up to 24hrs, then filtered with a 0.45 um syringe filter into combusted glass vials and kept on ice until refridgerated or frozen. Dissolved inorganic carbon samples were collected with no headspace in combusted 24mL vials and refridgerated until analyzed with a Shimadzu TOC using the IC method within one week of collection.
