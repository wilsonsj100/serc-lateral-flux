Dataset: 2025 FATE Monitoring Tidal Creek Surface Water DIC and DOC Concentrations - Monitoring wetland lateral fluxes at the Global Change Research Wetland (GCReW) Flume

Dataset Contacts: Stephanie Wilson (wilsonsj@si.edu), Amanda Reynolds (reynoldsa@si.edu), Patrick Megonigal (megonigalp@si.edu)

Any use of this dataset must include a citation. Dataset DOI: 10.25573/serc.34701435

The contents of this data publication are as follows:

DataDictionary.csv - Contains descriptive metadata for each column included in the data file. For each column units are clarified, variables are defined, data type is identified, and instrumentation and methodology used to determine the data are listed

FATE_Monitoring_DIC_DOC_2025.csv - Contains discrete sample monitoring of tidal creek surface water dissolved inorganic carbon, dissolved organic carbon, and total nitrogen concentrations at the Smithsonian Environmental Research Center GCReW Flume.

AuthorList.csv - Contains the list of dataset authors along with their ORCIDs, affiliations and emails.
