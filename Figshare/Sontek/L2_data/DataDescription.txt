These data are measurements of water temperature, depth, velocity, and discharge made at the Smithsonian Environmental Research Center's Global Change Research Wetland (GCReW) in the Kirkpatrick Marsh along the Rhode River in Edgewater, MD, USA. Measurements were made  with a Sontek IQ series acoustic doupler current profiler (ADCP) at the GCReW Flume (38.87486, -76.5465) through which a tidal creek flows. The Sontek ADCP is affixed to a PVC pipe bolted to the center of the flume that extended to just above the sediment surface. Measurements were collected every six minutes and recorded by a Campbell Scientific CR1000x logger as described by Wilson et al. (in review). These are L2 data, where L0 would be raw data is received by the logger from the instrument, L1 has been processed with programmatic quality checks for data out of the instrument bounds (OOR), out of service when the instrument is down for cleaning or servicing (OOS), and flagged for internal system status codes (SSF). L2 data have been visualized and flagged manually to identify data that is out of reasonalbe range (ORR). Water depth and index velocity measurements were manually QAQC'd, then those data, along with any dependent data (cross sectional area, mean velocity, flowrate) were made to be NA. The only exception are SSF flags 2 and 3, which indicate a change in tilt values that were input into the sontek and flag the user so that the IQ mount can be checked. Discharge, Q (m3/s), was calculated as the cross sectional area * velocity using both index velocity and mean velocity measured by the Sontek IQ and is reported here. The QAQC procedures are outlined and the code to produce these data from L0 are available on GitHub (link).
