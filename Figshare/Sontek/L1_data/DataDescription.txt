These data are measurements of water depth, velocity, and temperature made at the Smithsonian Environmental Research Center's Global Change Research Wetland (GCReW) in the Kirkpatrick Marsh along the Rhode River in Edgewater, MD, USA. Measurements were made with a Sontek IQ series acoustic doupler current profiler (ADCP) at the GCReW Flume (38.87486, -76.5465) through which a tidal creek flows. The Sontek ADCP is affixed to a PVC pipe bolted to the center of the flume that extended to just above the sediment surface. Measurements were collected every six minutes and recorded by a Campbell Scientific CR1000x logger as described by Wilson et al. (in review). These are L1 data, where L0 would be raw data that is received by the logger from the instrument and L1 has been processed with programmatic quality checks that flag data out that is out of the instrument bounds (OOR), out of service when the instrument is down for cleaning or servicing (OOS), and or flagged with internal system status codes (SSF).
